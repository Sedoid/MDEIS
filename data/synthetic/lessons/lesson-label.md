Objective: By the end of the lesson, learners can add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators.

## Introduction
Draw two strips of equal length on the board, one cut in thirds and one cut in quarters. Ask how we could add one third and one quarter.

## Activities
1. Show how to find a common denominator by listing multiples of 3 and 4.
2. Work two examples together on the board.
3. Learners do six practice sums alone. The weak ones will struggle with the common denominator, so I will sit them near the front and give them the easy sums while the rest do the harder set.
4. Early finishers check each other's work.

## Check for understanding
Three questions on the board. The slow ones can do just the first. I will mark them all and note who got none right.
