Objective: By the end of the lesson, learners can solve a one-step linear equation and check the answer by substitution.

## Introduction
Remind the class that an equation is a balance. Write x + 5 = 12 on the board and ask what number goes in the place of x.

## Activities
1. Learners say aloud the aim of the day: By the end of the lesson, learners can solve a one-step linear equation and check the answer by substitution.
2. Work three examples on the board: x + 5 = 12, y - 3 = 9 and 4n = 20.
3. Learners solve six equations alone and check each by putting their answer back in.
4. Pairs compare answers and settle any that differ.

## Check for understanding
Three equations on the board. Learners solve each and show the substitution. Restate the aim to close: By the end of the lesson, learners can solve a one-step linear equation and check the answer by substitution.
