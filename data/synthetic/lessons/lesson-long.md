Objective: By the end of the lesson, learners can read a passage closely, retell it in order and answer questions that need a detail from the text.

## Introduction
This is a double lesson with three parts. We begin with a short talk about reading closely. I will ask learners what they do when a passage is difficult, and write their answers on the board without comment. We then agree three habits for the day: read the passage twice, underline the words that answer the question, and say the answer back in a full sentence.

## Activities
1. Passage on a market day. Learners read it silently, then in pairs. They list who is buying, who is selling and what is traded. Each pair writes a retelling of three sentences in the right order, and underlines one detail that supports each sentence. Two pairs read out their retelling and the class decides whether the order and details match the passage.
2. Passage on a school library. Learners read it silently, then in pairs. They list where the books are kept and how a learner borrows one. ## Check for understanding
Give each learner a new passage of six lines. They retell it in three sentences in the correct order and answer two questions that each need one detail from the text. They copy the words from the passage that show where the answer came from. Collect the papers and read them before the next lesson.

## Notes for next lesson
Reread the retellings and look for places where the order was lost or a detail was missing. Plan a short review of those points at the start of the next lesson. Keep the same three habits on the board so that learners can use them again.
