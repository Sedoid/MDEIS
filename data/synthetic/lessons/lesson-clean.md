Objective: By the end of the lesson, learners can find the main idea of a short passage and support it with two details from the text.

## Introduction
Read aloud a short passage of about eight lines on a village market day. Ask what the passage is mostly about and write two or three answers on the board without comment.

## Activities
1. Learners read the passage again in pairs and underline the sentence that tells the main idea.
2. Each pair lists two details from the passage that support that main idea.
3. Two pairs read out their main idea and details. The class says whether each detail fits.
4. Learners write one sentence of their own that states the main idea of a second, shorter passage.

## Check for understanding
Give each learner a new three-line passage. They write the main idea in one sentence and copy one detail that supports it. Collect the papers at the door.
