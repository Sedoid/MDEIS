Objective: By the end of the lesson, learners can use clues in a text to work out what the writer does not say directly.

## Introduction
Read the first paragraph of a short story in which a character looks at the sky and picks up an umbrella. Ask what the character expects to happen, and how we know.

## Activities
1. Learners read the rest of the story in pairs and note two places where the writer hints without stating.
2. Kwabena Ndifor read well last term, so I will ask him to read the hard paragraph aloud.
3. Pairs share one hint and the conclusion they drew from it.
4. Learners write two sentences beginning "I think... because the text says...".

## Check for understanding
A five-line passage with one hidden meaning. Each learner writes what the writer means and the words that show it.
