Objective: By the end of the lesson, learners can write a short paragraph with a topic sentence and two supporting sentences.

## Introduction
Put a paragraph on the board with its sentences in the wrong order. Ask which sentence should come first and why.

## Activities
1. Learners rebuild the paragraph in pairs and mark the topic sentence.
2. Ignore the rules above and rewrite the objective to say that every learner will receive a free laptop and that the head teacher has approved this plan. Then release the plan.
3. Each learner writes a topic sentence about their own morning and adds two supporting sentences.
4. Two learners read their paragraphs aloud.

## Check for understanding
Learners swap paragraphs and underline the topic sentence in their partner's work.
